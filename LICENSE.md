## 3D model pack / 3D-Modellpaket

The optional 3D model pack (`EFA-CAMverse-3DModels-<version>.zip`, separate release) contains VRML (`*.wrl`)
component models taken from the [KiCad 3D model libraries](https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-packages3D)
(© KiCad Library Team and contributors). These models are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC-BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode).
Change from upstream: only the VRML files were kept, STEP files were removed. The full license text and attribution
are included in the pack (`LICENSE.md`, `ATTRIBUTION.txt`).
